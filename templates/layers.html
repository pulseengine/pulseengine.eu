{# ── the time machine (#205) ─────────────────────────────────────── #}
history · what changed, layer by layer

Layers, receding into the past

The front card is the layer you are reading; the cards behind it are what came before. Scrub with the ruler, the arrows, or the arrow keys. For an ordinary layer the diff is computed against the one behind it at two levels — tool versions, and per-platform payloads. The second level catches a layer that ships fewer builds than its predecessor without any version changing. covalent carries no payloads of its own, so it gets a view of its own.

{# Lines run in parallel, so chronology and line membership are two axes. The chart puts time on the horizontal and gives each line its own lane, which is the only shape that can show a layer cut into an older line after a newer line opened. #}

Time runs left to right and each line has its own lane. The solid bar is the span a line received deposits; the lighter tail is how long its newest layer stays supported. Two lanes overlapping is two lines supported at once, which is the normal state once a new line opens.

Reading the provenance column. cosign-sums and build-provenance mean a signature or an attestation covers the bytes, and the row says what it asserts. unverified means nothing vouched for them: the payload was ingested on an explicit operator decision, and the reason is signed into the layer alongside it. Showing that number is the point. A view that could only report green would not be evidence.
